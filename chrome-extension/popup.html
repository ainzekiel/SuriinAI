Privacy Policy & Data Agreement

1. Introduction

Welcome to Suriin AI. We protect your personal information in compliance with the Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: Name, email, and encrypted passwords for authentication.

Scanned Content: Text, links, or messages you submit or the extension scans via your selected mode (Auto, Manual, or Highlight).

Suriin AI does not secretly read or log your private conversations. Data is only processed when the extension is active.

3. How We Use Your Information

To analyze text and URLs using our AI model for scam risk scores, route high-risk reports to verified Cybersecurity Experts, and display your scan history on your dashboard.

4. Data Security & Storage

All data is transmitted securely and stored using Google Firebase. Only authorized Experts can view flagged reports — they cannot see your passwords.

5. Your Rights (RA 10173)

Be Informed — Know exactly how your data is used.

Access & Correct — View your reports and update your profile.

Erase — Request full deletion of your account and scan data via Settings.

6. Contact Us

Questions? Contact the Suriin AI Capstone Development Team via the web app.

By logging in, you agree to our Privacy Policy and consent to data processing under RA 10173.
