Help & Getting Started

Learn how to install the Suriin AI Chrome Extension and link it to your dashboard.

Setup Guide

1

Install the Suriin AI Chrome Extension

Download the Suriin AI Scanner extension from the Chrome Web Store or load it manually in Developer Mode. Navigate to chrome://extensions, enable "Developer mode" in the top-right corner, then click "Load unpacked" and select the extension folder.

2

Pin the Extension to Your Toolbar

Click the puzzle piece icon (🧩) in the Chrome toolbar, find Suriin AI Scanner, and click the pin icon to keep it visible. This gives you quick access to the extension popup.

3

Log In with Your Suriin AI Account

Open the extension popup by clicking its icon. Enter the same email and password you used to register on this dashboard. This links your browser scans to your account so reports appear in My Reports.

4

Browse Normally — The Scanner Runs Automatically

Once logged in, the extension monitors web pages in the background. When it detects suspicious content — phishing pages, investment scams, fake job postings — it automatically extracts the text, analyzes it using AI-powered heuristics, and submits a report.

5

Review Reports on Your Dashboard

Head to My Reports to see all flagged content. Each report shows a risk score, risk factors, and the extracted text. Click "View" to see full details, or download a PDF Evidence file for your records.

Understanding Risk Scores

Every scanned page receives a Risk Score between 0% and 100%, calculated using natural language processing (NLP) heuristics. The score reflects how many scam indicators were found in the extracted text.

  • 0–39% (Safe) — No significant scam indicators detected. The content appears legitimate.
  • 40–69% (Warning) — Some suspicious elements found. Exercise caution and verify the source.
  • 70–100% (Critical) — Multiple strong scam indicators detected. Avoid interacting with this content.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the extension slow down my browser?

No. Suriin AI Scanner is lightweight and runs asynchronously. Text extraction happens in the background without affecting page load times. The extension only activates on pages with detectable text content.

Can I scan a page manually?

Yes. Open the extension popup and click the "Scan This Page" button. The scan results will appear in the popup and a report will be submitted to your dashboard.

What happens after I submit a report?

Reports are stored securely in the Suriin AI database. Expert investigators (law enforcement, cybersecurity analysts) review flagged content and may update the report status to "Under Investigation" or "Resolved."

Is my personal information shared with anyone?

No. Your identity is protected. Reports are associated with your account ID only, and investigators cannot see your personal details. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I uninstall the extension?

Right-click the Suriin AI icon in your toolbar and select "Remove from Chrome", or go to chrome://extensions and click "Remove." Your dashboard account and existing reports will remain unaffected.

I need more help. How can I contact support?
