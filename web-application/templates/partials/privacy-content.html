Suriin AI - Privacy Policy & Data Agreement

Effective Date: April 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Suriin AI. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data in compliance with the Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (DPA).

2. Information We Collect

To provide our scam detection services, we collect the following:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and encrypted passwords used for authentication.
  • Scanned Content: Text, links, or messages that you explicitly submit or that the extension scans via your selected scanning mode (Automatic, Manual, or Highlight).

Note: Suriin AI does not secretly read or log your private conversations. Data is only processed when the extension is active and investigating potential threats.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To analyze text and URLs using our AI model to determine scam risk scores.
  • To route flagged, high-risk reports to verified Cybersecurity Experts for manual review.
  • To display your scan history and personalized cybersecurity metrics on your user dashboard.

4. Data Security and Storage

All data is transmitted securely and stored using Google Firebase, which complies with top-tier international security standards. We employ strict access controls; only authorized Experts can view flagged reports, and they cannot see your personal account passwords.

5. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under RA 10173, you possess the right to:

  • Be Informed: Knowing exactly how your data is used (as outlined here).
  • Access & Correct: View your submitted reports and update your profile details.
  • Erase: Request the complete deletion of your account and all associated scan data via the "Settings" page.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data rights, please contact the Suriin AI Capstone Development Team.
