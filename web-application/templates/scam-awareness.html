Scam Awareness

Learn about common online scam tactics targeting Filipinos and how to protect yourself.

Phishing Scams

Phishing is the most common form of cyber fraud. Scammers send emails, text messages (smishing), or social media messages impersonating legitimate organizations like banks, government agencies, or popular services to trick you into revealing personal information.

They often create fake websites that look identical to real ones, urging you to "verify your account" or "claim a prize." Once you enter your credentials, the scammer gains access to your accounts.

🚩 Urgent "act now" language 🚩 Suspicious sender address 🚩 Misspelled URLs 🚩 Requests for passwords 🚩 Generic greetings

Investment & Ponzi Scams

These scams promise unusually high returns with little or no risk. They often use social media testimonials, fake screenshots of earnings, and pressure tactics to recruit new investors. Common types include cryptocurrency scams, forex trading schemes, and pyramid/Ponzi structures.

In the Philippines, these scams frequently operate through Facebook groups and messaging apps, often disguised as "investment cooperatives" or "trading academies."

🚩 "Guaranteed" high returns 🚩 Pressure to recruit others 🚩 Unregistered with SEC 🚩 Vague business model 🚩 Difficulty withdrawing funds

Romance Scams

Scammers create fake profiles on dating sites and social media platforms to build emotional relationships with victims. Over weeks or months, they develop trust before fabricating emergencies — medical bills, travel expenses, or business troubles — to request money.

These scams are particularly harmful because victims often feel ashamed and don't report them. The emotional manipulation makes it difficult to recognize the scam even when warned.

🚩 Refuses video calls 🚩 Claims to be overseas 🚩 Financial emergencies 🚩 Too-perfect profile 🚩 Asks for gift cards/crypto

Job & Recruitment Scams

Fake job postings promise high-paying work-from-home positions with minimal qualifications. Victims may be asked to pay "processing fees," purchase equipment, or provide sensitive personal information like IDs and bank details during a fake onboarding process.

Some sophisticated variants involve fake "task-based" jobs where victims are asked to complete simple online tasks for small payments, then pressured into depositing larger amounts to "unlock higher-paying" tasks.

🚩 Upfront payment required 🚩 Unrealistic salary offers 🚩 No real company website 🚩 Vague job descriptions 🚩 Contact via Telegram only

Tech Support Scams

Scammers pose as technical support from well-known companies (Microsoft, Apple, Google) claiming your device is infected with a virus or has been compromised. They use pop-up warnings, phone calls, or emails to create urgency.

Victims are tricked into granting remote access to their computers, where scammers install malware, steal data, or charge for unnecessary "repair" services.

🚩 Unsolicited calls 🚩 Pop-up virus warnings 🚩 Requests remote access 🚩 Payment via gift cards 🚩 Creates false urgency
